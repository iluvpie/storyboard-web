    Backups

    Every save keeps a version here, inside the app, not in your folders. Recent ones are all kept, then one an hour for a day, then one a day for two weeks. Opening one makes a copy; your board's file is never changed.

    , .
    Previous or next shot
    Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V
    Copy the selected layers (or the lasso selection) and paste them into any shot
    V
    Move: drag to move the layer (or the selection); drag a handle to stretch
    Shift
    Like Photoshop. Brush: drag to keep a line level or upright. Move: keeps proportions, or locks the direction. Grid points: straight lines
    Alt / Ctrl
    Move: Alt stretches from the centre, Ctrl places without snapping. Timeline: Ctrl skips snapping
    = and -
    Zoom the timeline in and out (or Ctrl/Alt + scroll); \ fits it
    Arrows
    With Move, or a lasso selection: nudge it (Shift for 10px)
    Shift / Ctrl + lasso
    Add another area to the selection, or cut one out of it (both: keep only the overlap). Undo takes selections back.
